What to expect

  • Idle: the pencil follows the pointer, tip on the hotspot, no bobbing.
  • Hover: one sparkle when you enter an interactive element, not a stream.
  • Press: the pencil taps 2px and a burst of 6 pixels flies from the click point.
  • Touch: on a phone or tablet the native pointer stays; no pencil is drawn.
  • Reduced motion: sparkles and flying particles are off; a short static flash remains.
